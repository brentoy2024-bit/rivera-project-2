Welcome to Kabankalan Catholic College

Kabankalan Catholic College is an educational institution committed to providing quality education rooted in Christian values, academic excellence, and holistic student development.

  • Promotes academic excellence through quality education and effective learning experiences.
  • Develops students with strong Christian values, discipline, respect, and responsibility.
  • Encourages students to develop their talents, leadership skills, and abilities to become responsible individuals who can contribute positively to society.
