About Us

Welcome to Kabankalan Catholic College! We are committed to providing quality education guided by faith, excellence, discipline, and Christian values. Our goal is to develop knowledgeable, responsible, and compassionate individuals prepared for the future.

Kabankalan Catholic College

Education that inspires, faith that guides, and values that last a lifetime.

Kabankalan Catholic College is dedicated to providing students with meaningful educational experiences. The institution promotes academic excellence, spiritual development, leadership, and social responsibility.

  • Promotes quality education and academic excellence.
  • Develops students with strong Christian values.
  • Encourages leadership, discipline, and responsibility.
  • Builds responsible individuals who contribute to society.

Who We Are

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality and holistic education rooted in Christian values, developing responsible, competent, and compassionate individuals who serve God and society.

Our Vision

To become a Christ-centered educational institution that promotes academic excellence, strengthens faith, and develops individuals who make a positive difference in the community.
